# Code of Conduct

## Our goal

Cloud Native Ilorin is a community for people who want to learn, build, share knowledge, and contribute to open source.

We want this project to be a place where people feel welcome, regardless of their experience level.

You don't need to be an expert to participate.

## Be respectful

When contributing to this project, please:

* Treat other people with respect.
* Be patient with beginners.
* Ask questions and be open to questions.
* Give constructive feedback.
* Explain disagreements without attacking people.
* Give credit to other people's work.
* Keep discussions focused on helping the project and its contributors.

## Keep it welcoming

Everyone comes to open source with different levels of experience.

Someone may be making their first GitHub contribution while someone else has been contributing for years.

That's okay.

We encourage people to:

* Ask questions when they are stuck.
* Share what they know.
* Help others learn.
* Admit when they don't know something.
* Learn from mistakes and feedback.

There is no expectation that you should know everything before contributing.

## What isn't acceptable

The following behavior is not welcome:

* Harassment or personal attacks.
* Discrimination or hateful comments.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions.
* Sharing someone's private information without permission.
* Any other behavior that makes the community unsafe or hostile.

## When something goes wrong

If you experience or witness behavior that goes against this Code of Conduct, please let the project maintainers know.

You can contact the Cloud Native Ilorin team through the community's official contact channels.

When reporting an issue, provide as much context as you're comfortable sharing.

Reports will be handled respectfully and as privately as reasonably possible.

## Our responsibility

Project maintainers are responsible for helping keep the project welcoming and addressing behavior that goes against these guidelines.

We may take appropriate action when necessary, including asking someone to stop a behavior, removing inappropriate contributions, or restricting participation in the project.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether something is appropriate, it's usually better to ask.

We're here to learn and build together.

**Learn. Build. Contribute.**

— Cloud Native Ilorin
